TimeFrame

Find a time that works for everyone.

TimeFrame reads when you're busy from Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Notion or a calendar file, merges it with your group's schedules and ranks the best meeting times. It only uses when you're busy (read-only), never what your events are, and never stores event titles.

  1. Connect a calendar, or add busy times by hand.
  2. Share a group link with friends or teammates.
  3. Pick from the free slots that suit everyone.
